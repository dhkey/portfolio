title: Future of Coding
date: 2026-10-31
summary: What do I think about the future of coding and software development in general.
---

Recently, I recalled what it was like working as a freelancer three years ago.
I was only 16. I became brave enough to start developing software for a living.
My first order was a simple website, and the point was to create a small PHP page 
that would display to the user an output in real time (i hated websockets at that time). 
I earned 5$ from this job. I was absolutely thrilled to be able to make money without even leaving
my home - and to do it by using my own knowledge, specifically by doing something I love.

Why I am telling you about this? A couple of months later, I landed a very big order.
A massive website with a high amount of API calls to other services, a payment gateway, 
user authentication, and much more. I was just a kid, 16 years old, there was no such thing as
"Claude Code", there was NO AI in the way we know it today now at all. And that is precisely 
waht surprises me - I built this website in two month, entirely on my own (except some frontend and design). 
All I used were Youtube videos and some docs. There were so many problems I had to solve, and at the time, 
I didn't really have much experience. For sure, I knew how to code... but I hadn't ventured beyond SQLite3 or 
the Flask framework, for example. 

But I found a way out. I have always known how to wriggle out of a tight spot. I was in the 9th grate 
at the time. I called two classmates of mine (we were in a computer science-focused track at scholl).
First one - was good at the design, second - great frontend developer. But the biggest problem lay 
elsewere - the backend, the database, and so on. So I began to cook. I learnt the nuances of web 
development - how to create secure websites, and, most important how to do so on a high standart.
I've gone through the entire journey - from creating a "Hello World!" page to building an 
authorization system, and from watching "why users can't access localhost" to deploying nginx
on a amazon aws ec2 server. I got such a dopamine rush when everything started up, I can't even 
describe.

The point of this blog - I regret that I will never experience this feeling again. The problem lies
with AI. Requirenments for developers have changed. I spent two month for development of that web.
Novadays, to compete with others - I need to do it in a three days. What is almost impossible to do 
without AI. We are forced to use it to speed up the development, but it's a slippery slope sometimes.
Because before you know it - you start using it for everything. I can say it for sure, I see how things
are going on in my university. 99% can't even write bubble sort without an AI. 
Yeah, it helps - for sure, I use it myself very often. But I honestly fell bad when I look at all this generated code.

To make this blog lives up to its name - real developers, I mean not "vibecoders", will still 
be really needed for at least another couple of years. Times are changing for our profession, and
very, very, very quickly. The only thing we can do - learn, learn and learn. Write code by hand,
read books, keep developing. Just keep do what you like, develop. Develop your future, don't let 
AI to do it.
